Ministering Church
Building the shape of ministry

Worship and camps — preparing the places where people gather.

03
Sound System Setup
A sanctuary where the sermon is heard clearly. We assess the room, choose and install the equipment, and set up the mix for online streaming too.
04
AKC — Arise Korea Camp
A camp that wakes the next generation and young adults of this land. Held over the summer and winter breaks, with churches gathering together — so even a church with few young people can join simply by registering.

Boards where churches
meet each other directly

The Center opens the boards and checks what goes up. Posting and browsing is done by the churches themselves.

Church PropertyChurches letting go of a worship space meet churches looking for one. Only posts with verified ownership documents go up, and they stay listed until the deal closes.
Ministry Job PostingsStipend, housing and working conditions must be stated — the reader has to be able to judge. Once you have filled the post, the church takes it down.
Used Equipment MarketChurch equipment and goods change hands. If the buying church wants it, the Center handles removal, transport, installation and sound tuning.
Church GuesthouseOnly rooms offered free of charge and facilities registered as lodging are listed. Guests and churches contact each other directly.
Event TicketingSet the time booking opens and it closes automatically when full. Seat selection is optional, and the organiser downloads the list of registrants.

The Center operates the boards only — it does not act as a broker under the Licensed Real Estate Agents Act, and takes no part in negotiating price or terms, or in contracts. For ministry job postings the Center is not the employer; interviews and calling decisions are made by the church and the minister directly. Listings, goods and postings are written by whoever posts them.
