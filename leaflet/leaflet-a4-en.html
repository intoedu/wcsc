How a request moves

One request form. The Center takes it from there.

1
Submit the form
Pick the items you need on our website and leave a request. You get a reference number straight away.
2
Call and consultation
We get in touch within 1–2 business days. We can also visit you.
3
Proposal and quote
You receive a proposal with scope, schedule and cost, plus the documents you need for an internal decision.
4
Work and launch
We work to the agreed schedule, check in with you partway, and hand it over for real use.
5
Support afterwards
Fixes, checks and questions are supported through the support period. Add more items any time.
Boards where churches meet each other directly — open to you with no support item requested
Church Property
Churches letting go of a worship space meet churches looking for one. Only posts with verified ownership documents go up, and they stay listed until the deal closes.
Ministry Job Postings
Stipend, housing and working conditions must be stated — the reader has to be able to judge. Once you have filled the post, the church takes it down.
Used Equipment Market
Church equipment and goods change hands. If the buying church wants it, the Center handles removal, transport, installation and sound tuning.
Church Guesthouse
Only rooms offered free of charge and facilities registered as lodging are listed. Guests and churches contact each other directly.
Event Ticketing
Set the time booking opens and it closes automatically when full. Seat selection is optional, and the organiser downloads the list of registrants.

The Center operates the boards only — it does not act as a broker under the Licensed Real Estate Agents Act, and takes no part in negotiating price or terms, or in contracts. For ministry job postings the Center is not the employer; interviews and calling decisions are made by the church and the minister directly. Listings, goods and postings are written by whoever posts them.
